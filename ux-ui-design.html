UX/UI Designer Intern

Craft Exceptional User Experiences for Games

  • จำนวนรับ: 2 ตำแหน่ง
  • ระยะเวลา: Flexible (ยืดหยุ่นตามภาคเรียน)
  • สถานที่: Hybrid Work (สลับทำงานที่สำนักงานและที่บ้าน)
  • เวลาทำงาน: จันทร์-ศุกร์ 09:00-18:00 น.

เกี่ยวกับตำแหน่ง

Our mission is to craft exceptional experiences for game players, starting from brainstorming features and proposing innovative ideas, to designing wireframes and flows. We serve as the cohesive element that connects the efforts of all departments, ensuring the overall aesthetic and harmony of the game.

คุณสมบัติ

  • Methodologies: Design Theory, UX Laws (Gestalt, Hick's, Fitts's)
  • Hard Skills: Figma / Sketch / Adobe XD (Preferred), Adobe Illustrator (Required), Adobe Photoshop (Basic)
  • Preferred: Atomic Design, Data Analysis, Unity, Basic Programming, SQL

ความรับผิดชอบหลัก

  • Gather requirements from the Mentor for feature design.
  • Design low-fidelity wireframes (depending on the needs of the task).
  • Refine and revise based on feedback from the Mentor to create high-fidelity wireframes.
  • Design game assets to support the display at various points while maintaining aesthetic quality.
  • Participate in gathering feedback from users, whether they are internal or external.

สิ่งที่จะได้เรียนรู้

  • Get familiar with Figma to create wireframes and build design components.
  • Learn how to think like a UX Designer, including conducting usability tests.
  • Learn the basic knowledge for designing responsive layout/devices.
  • Refine the game assets to meet industry standards.

Software และ Tools ที่ใช้

Software: Figma, Google suite, Microsoft Office, Adobe program, Autodesk program, Blender, Unity, Unreal engine
