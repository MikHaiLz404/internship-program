Animator Intern

Bring Games to Life with Animation

General Information

  • Positions: 1 position
  • Duration: Flexible
  • Location: Hybrid Work
  • Working Hours: Mon-Fri 09:00-18:00

About the Role

Our Animation Department brings games to life by creating all the moving elements you see and interact with during gameplay. We work on everything from character animations and creature movements to UI transitions, in-game cinematics, and interactive game assets.

Work Environment

  • Collaborate closely with cross-functional teams and provide troubleshooting support when technical issues arise during production.
  • We maintain rigorous quality control processes, thoroughly reviewing every piece of work before delivery to ensure seamless handoffs to other teams.
  • Daily activities include: gathering reference materials, team discussions and planning, hands-on animation work, incorporating feedback through iterative revisions until approval, and implementing animations in-game for testing and validation.

Qualifications

  • Solid understanding of fundamental animation principles
  • Strong observational skills and attention to detail
  • Effective communication abilities with team members
  • Proactive approach to asking questions and seeking clarification
  • Eagerness and adaptability to learn new techniques and workflows
  • Creative problem-solving mindset

Key Responsibilities

  • Create animations for characters, creatures, objects, and various game elements based on assignments and guidance provided by mentors for each practice task.

Learning Objectives

  • Develop foundational animation skills that can be applied effectively in professional production environments.

Tools & Technologies

Software: Google suite, Microsoft Office, Adobe program, Autodesk program, Blender, Unity, Unreal engine
