Department information                       Your department                     *           Dropdown           UX/UI                       Department description                     *                     Brief description of what your department does, main responsibilities, and current projects           Our UX/UI Design department is part of the Design team, which includes both Game Designers and UX/UI Designers.Our mission is to craft exceptional experiences for game players, starting from brainstorming features and proposing innovative ideas, to designing wireframes and flows that align with the requirements established by the Game Designers. We also focus on designing game assets and arranging everything within layouts compatible with various devices.We serve as the cohesive element that connects the efforts of all departments, ensuring the overall aesthetic and harmony of the game.Currently, we are diligently developing a project titled Regulus - The Advent.                       Work environment                     Team culture, collaboration style, typical day activities, etc           The work of the UX Designers begins by listening to ideas from Game Designers and players. We discuss the origins and potential limitations of each feature with Technical Artists and Developers. Then, we start designing wireframes and flows that support responsive layouts across various devices, keeping user usability as our primary focus. We also establish a design system structure for UI Designers to utilize. Once this is done, we hand off our work to the UI Designers, who begin designing the presentation of various outputs that align with the specified data set while maintaining the game's aesthetic and adhering to the design system established by the UX Designers.After all steps have been reviewed by relevant stakeholders, the final phase involves creating main components for all game assets to facilitate future modifications or changes before passing the work on to other teams.It may seem like the project is complete, but not quite yet! Our work also includes testing and gathering feedback from players, which we analyze and summarize to improve each feature further.By the time we finish all these steps, we will have communicated with nearly everyone involved in the game! Therefore, the key to our success is simply: empathy.As for what we do in our free time? We enjoy sipping tea and chatting with various colleagues about work or games. We stay updated on general news, as it helps us understand everyone better—it's not just curiosity! And in our real free time (?), we play video games or board games with everyone in the office. After all, working at a game company without playing anything would be quite strange!
