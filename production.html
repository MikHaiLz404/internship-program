Production Intern

Game Production & Project Management

ข้อมูลทั่วไป

  • จำนวนรับ: 2 ตำแหน่ง
  • ระยะเวลา: 4 เดือน (ยืดหยุ่นตามภาคเรียน)
  • สถานที่: Hybrid Work (สลับทำงานที่สำนักงานและที่บ้าน)
  • เวลาทำงาน: จันทร์-ศุกร์ 09:00-18:00 น.

เกี่ยวกับตำแหน่ง

The management and coordination of all aspects of developing a game project. Ensure the game is completed on time, within budget, and meets quality standards.

คุณสมบัติ

  • Communication & Collaboration – Strong communication, active listening, note-taking, and confidence to ask questions.
  • Organization & Time Management – Ability to prioritize tasks, manage time effectively, and pay attention to detail.
  • Analytical & Problem-Solving Mindset – Logical thinking, basic analytical skills, and initiative to research solutions.
  • Adaptability & Resilience – Flexibility with changing priorities, patience with the learning curve, and a positive attitude under pressure.
  • Growth-Oriented Mindset – Willingness to learn from feedback, and genuine interest in project management.

ความรับผิดชอบหลัก

  • Assist in meeting preparation, take minutes, and track key action items.
  • Help maintain project schedules, calendars, and coordination tasks.
  • Create and update project status reports using company templates.
  • Support data entry, file organization, and document management.

สิ่งที่จะได้เรียนรู้

  • Understand the organization, projects life cycle, and professional workplace culture.
  • Gain hands-on experience with project management tools and systems.
  • Develop strong skills in communications across the team, reporting, and project documentation.
  • Build a solid foundation in project management practices and fundamentals.
  • Grow through real responsibilities, teamwork, and supervised project tasks.

Software และ Tools ที่ใช้

Software: Google suite, Microsoft Office, Jira, Confluence
